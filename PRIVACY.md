# Privacy Policy for Molkky Score

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Overview

Molkky Score is a score-tracking application for the game of Molkky. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Molkky Score does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any other contact information
- We do not collect location data
- We do not use analytics or tracking services
- We do not share any data with third parties
- All game data (player names, scores) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted

## In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional premium subscription (300 JPY/month) through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase process: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data Storage

Any data you enter in the App (such as player names and game scores) is stored locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage). This data does not leave your device.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
